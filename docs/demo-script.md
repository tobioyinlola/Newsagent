# Cover Founder: investor demo (about 3½ minutes)

Screen-recorded walkthrough of the real product with live clicks, a highlighted cursor and a natural voice-over.
Voice: warm, confident, conversational (OpenAI TTS, e.g. "nova" or "alloy"). About 150 words a minute.

| # | Screen and actions | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Home page** (coverfounder.com). Slow scroll past the hero, the Business Interviews row and Top stories. | "Every week, thousands of startups in Africa launch products, raise money and hire, and almost none of it gets covered. Media coverage is expensive, slow, and built for big companies. Cover Founder changes that. We're an AI newsroom that turns any founder's announcement into a broadcast-quality news interview, a press release, and real media coverage, in minutes, not weeks." |
| 2 | **Sign up / dashboard.** Click "Publish your free story!", land on the dashboard. | "A founder signs up in under a minute. Their first story is free." |
| 3 | **Tell us your news.** Pick "Funding round", paste a few lines about the raise, name and role, then Continue. | "They tell us what's happening, a funding round, a launch, a partnership, in their own words. Our AI reads it, works out the company, the news and who it matters to, and prepares the interview questions." |
| 4 | **AI interview room.** Countdown, the founder answers on camera, a timer, "I'm done answering", next question. | "Then the founder is interviewed by our AI presenter, right in the browser. Four questions, about a minute each. No studio, no crew, no scheduling. Every answer is checked as it's recorded: if it's too dark, too quiet or too short, they simply try again." |
| 5 | **Your press release.** Scroll the generated release, the opening, the closing remark and sign-off. | "From the interview, Cover Founder writes a wire-style press release using only the facts and the founder's own words. Anything we can't verify is flagged, never invented. The founder reviews it, and nothing is paid for until they approve." |
| 6 | **Make my video, then progress, then the finished video plays** (real customer video). | "Then we produce the news segment. Our AI presenter opens the show and welcomes the founder by name, asks each question, and the founder's own answers play in between, with name cards, headlines and their website on screen. It's a real newsroom format, consistent on every video, made automatically." |
| 7 | **Download and share.** HD and Light downloads, share link, the public story page on Featured Stories. | "The founder gets the video in HD and a lighter version for phones, a share link, and a public story page on Cover Founder, ready for LinkedIn, X and WhatsApp." |
| 8 | **Press distribution.** Choose outlets (TechCabal, BusinessDay and more), see credit prices, pay from the wallet. | "And it doesn't stop there. In a few clicks, founders send their story to real publications across Africa, priced per outlet and paid from their credit wallet. Our team handles the outreach and tracks each placement until it's published." |
| 9 | **Close** on the home page / logo. | "Cover Founder: every founder's story, told like the news. Fast, affordable, and built to scale across Africa and beyond." |

Word count of the voice-over: about 480 (≈ 3 min 15 s at 150 wpm), plus pauses for the video clips.

Production notes
- Recorded at 1920×1080 with a visible cursor and a soft highlight on each click; captions burned in as a fallback.
- Scene 6 shows a real finished customer video (with the founder's permission), not a stand-in.
- Customer-side screens only (no back office). Scenes 1, 7 and part of 8 are recorded on the live site; scenes 2–6 and the outlet picker on a demo account so no real customer data is shown and nothing is charged.
