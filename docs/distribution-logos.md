# Distribution picker logos

Publisher logo/official site-icon assets are served locally from `frontend/public/distribution-logos`; Business Insider reuses the existing public-page logo. Source URLs, publisher websites and image dimensions are recorded in `frontend/src/lib/distributionLogos.json`. White wordmarks use a dark tile to preserve their original artwork and contrast. Files are unmodified publisher assets.

The picker keeps outlet names, credits, categories, selection, drafts and checkout behavior unchanged. Logos are decorative beside their visible names. Missing or failed images use an initials badge; unverified brand marks are never fabricated or assigned from an unrelated site.

The following eight entries currently use initials because the publisher identity/logo could not be verified from reachable public sources:

- Celeb Spotlight
- Iconic Africa
- Iconic Global
- MP3Naija
- Naijamusic
- News Nine
- P mp Kulture
- Pilot Newspaper

Some candidate domains were parked, inactive or redirected to unrelated content; none of those assets were included. Replace an initials badge by adding a verified local image and its provenance to the manifest. The full name and selection remain available meanwhile.

The shared media-logo marquee scrolls continuously at a slow speed. It has no pause/resume control or hover pause. Reduced-motion preferences retain a static layout.
